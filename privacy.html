Hosting by GitHub Pages

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and your browser type (server log files). GitHub needs this data to deliver the website and to keep it secure. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing this website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Because GitHub is based in the USA, data may be transferred there. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). GitHub decides how long log data is kept; details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

All other content of this website, including fonts, scripts and images, is served from this website itself. No content is loaded from other third-party servers, apart from the visitor statistics described below.

Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To see how often this website is visited and how visitors find it, I use GoatCounter (goatcounter.com), an open-source web analytics service operated by Martin Tournoij in Ireland. Its data is stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany and Finland, so no data leaves the EU.

When you open a page, the following data is sent to GoatCounter: the page visited, the referring website, your browser and operating system with their versions, your browser language, your screen width and your IP address. GoatCounter uses your IP address only to derive your country and, together with your browser identification, to recognise repeat visits; it keeps this in memory for at most 8 hours and never stores your IP address. GoatCounter does not set cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not create user profiles. I receive only aggregated statistics, for example the number of visitors per day, countries and referring websites, and I cannot identify you from them.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in understanding how this website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time (see "Right to object" below), and you can also prevent the statistics with any common content blocker. Further details are in GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use cookies, advertising or social-media plugins. If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that the page keeps it on your next visit. This information stays on your device, is not sent to me or to anyone else, and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings. Saving it is strictly necessary to provide the function you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Contact by email

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply to you. I do not pass them on to anyone else and delete them once they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your message relates to a contract or pre-contractual steps, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR otherwise.

External links

This website links to other websites such as SSRN, GitHub and LinkedIn. No data is sent to these websites until you click a link. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access your personal data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it rectified (Art. 16) or erased (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18) and to data portability (Art. 20). To exercise any of these rights, email me. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for me is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland, datenschutz.saarland.de.

Right to object

Where I process your data on the basis of my legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). An email to me is enough.

No obligation, no automated decisions

You are not obliged to provide any personal data to use this website. I do not use automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
