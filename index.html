The Sector Anatomy of Commodity Risk Premia

with Daniele Bianchi (Queen Mary University of London)
Revise & resubmit, Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis · Presented at GLOBAFA 2025
PDFSSRN
Splits commodity futures characteristics into a sector-wide and a within-sector part. The sector-wide part drives most of the return variance, but the premia that standard commodity factor models leave unexplained are earned within sectors.
Working paper2026

Diversification and Convexity in Trend Following

Sole author
PDF
Shows how the cross-sectional filter of a trend-following portfolio sets both its Sharpe ratio and its convexity. Across 73 futures markets, the equal-risk portfolio offers about fifteen independent Sharpe bets but only four convexity bets. A Sharpe-optimal filter estimated by risk-premium PCA raises the walk-forward Sharpe ratio from 0.60 to 1.33 with no detectable loss of convexity.
Working paper2026

Asymmetric Basis Momentum in Commodity Futures

with Björn Uhl (Universität Hamburg)
PDF
In 43 commodity futures over 1960–2024, only positive basis momentum predicts next-month returns; negative basis momentum does not. Separate slopes for the two sides earn an out-of-sample alpha of 0.30% per month beyond linear basis momentum and the commodity premium.
Working paper2026

The Anatomy of Factor Crowding: Volatility and Business-Cycle Risk

Sole author
PDF
Across 122 equity factors, the standard returns-based crowding score turns out to be mostly a volatility ranking (rank correlation 0.96). When the same decomposition targets the business cycle, it does tell us which factors will be most sensitive to the economy over the next two years.

The Limits of the Expectations Hypothesis

Sole author
PDF
An out-of-sample test of the expectations hypothesis on daily US repo rates (1998–2026), with linear, tree-based and neural-network forecasts. The departures are statistically predictable, but trading on them earns at most 2.2 basis points a year before costs.
